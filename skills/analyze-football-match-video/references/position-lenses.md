# Position-specific lenses

Apply the common rubric first. Use these prompts to add role context, not to force every player into a fixed model.

## Fullback or wing-back

- Buildup height and width; timing to drop, hold, invert, overlap, or underlap.
- Receiving shape near the touchline and access to inside or forward options.
- Relationship with winger, centre-back, and holding midfielder.
- Timing and quality of crosses, cutbacks, switches, and combinations.
- Pressing angle, protection of the inside channel, back-post awareness, and recovery line.
- Rest defence when the opposite side attacks.

## Centre-back

- Spacing and cover with the partner and goalkeeper.
- Scanning beyond the direct opponent and control of depth.
- Choice to step, delay, track, or hand over.
- First-line progression, carrying, and risk under pressure.
- Box positioning, aerial duels, second balls, and defensive-line leadership.

## Defensive or central midfielder

- Availability behind or beside the first press.
- Scanning frequency, half-turn receiving, and protection of the ball.
- Control of tempo, switches, line-breaking actions, and third-player combinations.
- Position relative to the ball for rest defence.
- Screening, counterpressing, runner tracking, and access to second balls.

## Attacking midfielder or winger

- Occupation of pockets, width, and blind-side positions.
- Timing of movements between feet and space.
- Isolation choices, combinations, carries, and final action.
- Box arrival and far-post behaviour.
- Counterpress reaction, recovery responsibility, and ability to direct the opponent.

## Striker

- Pinning, separation, checking movements, and depth threats.
- Link play, layoff quality, and timing of third-player runs.
- Box positioning relative to ball, defenders, and likely delivery.
- Shot selection and action speed.
- Pressing trigger, curved run, cover shadow, and connection to the block.

## Goalkeeper

- Starting position relative to the ball, line, and space behind the defence.
- Set position, handling, footwork, and decision to claim, punch, or hold.
- Sweeping choices and communication.
- Distribution selection, execution, and effect on team shape.
- Reset speed after the first action and set-piece organization.
