# Report templates

## Full review

### Scope and confidence

State the source, target player, playing intervals, timebase, coverage method, identity confidence, and major limitations in two or three sentences.

### Coverage audit

State the block size, reviewed blocks over total blocks, whether both the identity and action passes are complete, user markers reconciled, same-type rescans triggered by misses, and identity or visibility gaps. Use “complete” only when manifest validation allows it; otherwise call the report coverage-limited and name the affected ranges.

### Direct conclusion

Give a concise overall assessment. Name the strongest repeatable behaviour, the highest-value development mechanism, the direction from previous matches, and whether the evidence is sufficient for a stable conclusion.

### Complete player-involvement ledger

State the total number of confirmed sequence windows, uncertain exclusions, and any uncovered video ranges. Make this the primary chronological evidence table.

| # | Video range | Phase / labels | Observed involvement | Decision / execution / effect | Better option / coaching note | Confidence |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Include every confirmed attacking, defending, transition, restart, on-ball, and meaningful off-ball sequence, including routine positioning that directly shows the player's responsibility. Merge one continuous phase into one row; do not create one row per frame or include moments where the player is merely visible and uninvolved.

### Complete reception subset

State the number of confirmed receptions, uncertain exclusions, and any uncovered video ranges.

| # | Video time | Receive context | First touch and action | Outcome | Better option / coaching note | Confidence |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Include every confirmed reception, including routine safe possessions. Use “no clear better option” when the footage does not support an alternative. This is a training subset of the complete player-involvement ledger.

### Optional compact supporting table

| Video time | Phase | Observation | Decision / execution | Tactical effect | Confidence |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Use only when a short group of observations needs emphasis outside the main ledger. Do not move defensive or no-touch actions out of the main ledger merely because they are not receptions. Keep observation separate from interpretation and mark user-supplied timestamps.

### Verification of the player's notes

For each note, label it:

- Supported.
- Partly supported.
- Contradicted.
- Not judgeable.

Explain the decisive evidence without becoming defensive or flattering.

### Strengths to preserve

Use two or three evidence-backed strengths. Explain why each helps the team.

### Development priorities

Use two or three priorities in this form:

1. **Mechanism:** what repeats.
2. **Evidence:** representative timestamps.
3. **Cost:** what the mechanism changes tactically.
4. **Cue:** one short phrase usable during play.
5. **Practice:** task and constraint.
6. **Measure:** observable next-match indicator.

### Change from previous matches

| Tracked mechanism | Previous baseline | Current evidence | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use improved, resolved for now, stable, regressed, or inconclusive. Then state:

- Most important improvement.
- Most important regression, if any.
- Which old problem improved or remains unresolved.
- Any new issue.
- The single highest-value current priority.

### Three match-day cues

Give exactly three short cues, ordered by importance.

### Short training plan

Provide two or three sessions or modules. Include duration, setup, constraint, and success measure. Keep the plan proportional to the evidence.

### Uncertainty

List anything that could change the conclusion: incomplete footage, low identity confidence, off-camera context, unknown role instruction, or limited sample size.

## Compact clip review

Use when only a few clips are available:

1. Scope and limitations.
2. Every visible material player-involvement sequence in each clip, plus the reception subset when applicable.
3. What the clips do and do not support.
4. One strength.
5. One development mechanism.
6. Three cues only if the evidence is broad enough; otherwise give one provisional cue.

State explicitly that “all receptions” means all confirmed receptions visible inside the supplied clips, not all receptions from the match.

## Match comparison

Compare the same mechanisms rather than raw event counts alone:

| Mechanism | Previous evidence | Current evidence | Direction | Confidence | Context caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Classify direction as improved, resolved for now, stable, regressed, or inconclusive. Account for role, opponent, match state, relevant opportunity count, playing time, and coverage differences.

## Inaccessible or unusable video

Use a short failure report:

1. What source was tested and the specific access or evidence limitation.
2. What cannot be concluded, including any progress comparison.
3. The best permitted replacement: working link, full upload, or complete playing-interval clips.
4. Confirmation that no match result or baseline update was recorded.

Do not produce an empty performance grade or placeholder trend.
